What to track instead

Qualified opt-in rate.
Per page.

Opt-in rate tells you how many came in. Qualified rate tells you how many were worth having. Only one of them decides what to switch off.
You only get that number if you build it this way

Ask the qualifying question after they register.

Put it in front of registration and you lose people who would have signed up, and you never find out who they were.

Put it on the thank-you page and you keep them and still get the answer.
