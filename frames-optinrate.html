What I will not claim

This is a pattern on one account. It is not a law.

  • 01
    One client, one ad account, three launchesWhether it holds anywhere else is still an open question in our own notes.
  • 02
    Ad 3 spent £178 against Ad 4’s £1,647Seventeen qualified leads. Directional, not proof.

What to track instead
  • 01
    Qualified rate, per pageNot how many opted in. What share of them match who you can actually sell to.
  • 02
    Cost per qualified registration, per adOurs ran £8.00 on one launch and £11.02 on the next. Cost per lead said the opposite both times.
  • 03
    Both, side by side, per variantA page can win the first and lose the second. That is the only view that tells you what to switch off.
